Medical Literacy

Cancer Screening & Diagnostic Modalities

Interactive breakdown of standard clinical tests. Click any card for detailed medical insights.

🩻

1. Imaging Modalities

Visualizing structural abnormalities inside body tissues

Mammography & Ultrasound

Early detection of breast tissue density changes and soft tissue cysts.

X-Ray & Low-Dose CT Scan

Essential for lung, bone, and chest screening in high-risk individuals.

MRI Scan

High-resolution soft tissue contrast for neurological and abdominal areas.

🔬

2. Lab & Pathology

Cellular microscopic examination and bio-fluid analysis

Cytology (Pap Smear)

Microscopic cell scraping analysis for early cervical cancer detection.

Biopsy & Histopathology

The gold standard test. Examination of tissue samples under a microscope.

Blood Profile Tests

CBC and liver/kidney function screens to monitor overall biological health.

🧬

3. Advanced Molecular Testing

Genomic mutations and specific protein biomarkers

Biomarker Tests (PSA, CA-125)

Specific proteins produced by tumor cells detectable in blood serum.

Genetic Sequencing (BRCA1/2)

Identifies inherited gene mutations that elevate lifetime risk profiles.

Liquid Biopsy

Emerging technique catching circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA) fragments.

Project Highlights

What Makes OncoCare AI Innovative?

1
AI + Prevention Paradigm Shifts healthcare focus from late-stage diagnosis to proactive risk identification and lifestyle modification.
2
Digital App + Physical Kiosk Integration Combines mobile software accessibility with a tangible community health station model.
3
Multi-Factor Pattern Evaluation Evaluates how factors like tobacco and genetic history compound together rather than in isolation.
4
Scalable Social Impact Designed for deployment in schools, primary healthcare centers, civic camps, and rural clinics.
Scientific Responsibility

Limitations & Future Roadmap

⚠️ Prototype Acknowledgment

Our prototype uses simplified risk scoring to demonstrate AI pattern recognition concepts. It is not trained on validated clinical health datasets and does not replace professional medical diagnostics.
